## Changes from v2
- **Hook:** opens on the climax (the $3,000 silence) instead of a 7.6s audience call-out.
- **Promise:** the big promise is spoken by 0:20.
- **DUXIO:** named at 0:15.
- **Loop:** the "next DM" CTA closes the loop the hook opened.
- **The audit is sold:** what happens (map leaks live), what they get (fixes first) and no pitch.
- **Qualifier:** uses your numbers ($1,000+ offer, 20+ leads a month) and replaces "if you're just starting out".
- **Demo:** goes into second person ("you just take the call") so it stays a pitch to one person.
