# DUXIO VSL v2: visual treatment (faceless)

## The idea
Without a face, the trust has to come from three other places:
1. **The viewer's own world.** The whole video lives on the coach's phone and CRM, in real-looking UI. They should see their own DMs, their own 10 p.m.
2. **One lead, shown twice.** Follow one lead, Maya, through the coach's current process, where she goes cold. Then rewind and run the exact same DM through the Lead-to-Call System, where she books. That gives a before and after with no testimonial needed.
3. **A demonstration.** Show the actual system working: pipeline, replies, reminders, reactivation. Seeing exactly what they buy is the credibility.

## Visual system
- **Phone.** A large, brand-neutral iPhone-style frame (about 900px tall) in the left third. Hero type sits on the right. In the 9:16 recut the phone fills the screen.
- **Clock chip.** A persistent chip in the top left that narrates time, which is the villain: 2:14 PM → 10:02 PM → "3 days later" → after the rewind, "41s".
- **Chapter bar.** A thin top bar with four segments: The DM · The leak · The system · Your audit. It fills as the video plays, which helps viewers on VSL pages that can't scrub.
- **Palette.**
  - Daytime: DUXIO white and violet.
  - 10 p.m. onwards: the phone switches to dark mode on its own, with obsidian and red.
  - The rewind brings back daylight.
  - The CTA uses the obsidian brand look.
- **Type.**
  - Plus Jakarta 800, one hero line per frame.
  - Captions are always on, but hide while hero type is showing the same words.
- **Motion.**
  - UI moves the way UI moves: notifications drop with a spring; bubbles, cards and sheets push and slide.
  - Hard cuts on VO beats.
  - The only camera move is a motivated sideways track that follows Maya's card across the pipeline.
  - The only "effect" is the rewind.
  - No zooms.
- **Sound.**
  - SFX are phone sounds: ping, haptic buzz, typing clicks, send whoosh, calendar chime, call-join tone.
  - Music: a low pulse under the pain, total silence at "nothing", brighter on the rewind, a lift at the CTA.
- **Voice.** With no face, the voice carries the trust. Best option: the founder's own voice recorded on a phone in a quiet room; I clean it up. Fallback: a better TTS voice than the current one.

## Why this converts without a face
- **Identification replaces the face.** It is their phone, their DM, their 10 p.m., their no-show.
- **The same lead twice is the proof.** Viewers see one cause change one outcome, with no testimonials and no invented numbers.
- **The demo shows exactly what they buy.** The pipeline, the replies and the reminders are on screen, which removes the "what do I actually get?" doubt.
- **Loss made visible.** The racing clock, the music dropping to silence and the −$3,000 make the cost of inaction concrete.
- **A tangible CTA.** Viewers see the audit they'll receive and that it's free, which lowers the ask.
- **Orientation.** The chapter bar and the clock keep a faceless video easy to follow and watch to the end.
