# Concept Card: The Dots That Stopped (10s cut)

Built on `make-video/framework.md`. This is a 10 second cut of pilot #2 in `brand/duxio/docs/reels-30-plan.md`, which already went through ideation: 50 ideas were explored and 30 chosen.

- **Hook:** "They started typing. Then they stopped."
  - Frame 0: one giant typing bubble filling the screen width, with three dots pulsing like a heartbeat.
  - Framework hook shape 5, the viewer's own moment.
- **Story in one sentence:** the dots stop and fall, then one low-pressure message brings them back.
- **Spine:** question → show. Turn: "That's not a no." Callback: the dots hop back up into the same bubble, which is frame 0 again, so the reel loops.
- **Reference:**
  - v7 for the one-take camera and a single colour that means something (violet = our reply).
  - v5 for physical, tactile objects with a sound on every move.
  - v1 for the silence beat before the turn.
- **World:** a phone thread blown up to full frame on obsidian, with glass bubbles in grey (them) and violet (us). Captions come in short beats of up to three words, in white, low in the frame.
- **Quirk:** the three dots are physical. When the lead stops typing, they drop out of the bubble and bounce on the floor with tiny wooden plinks. At the end they hop back up into a new bubble.

## Five senses
| Sense | Plan |
|---|---|
| Sight | One giant bubble, obsidian and violet, the frame changes state about every 1.2s |
| Hearing | Heartbeat pulse synced to the dots, a hard cut to silence, plinks in the silence, typing keys, the send whoosh, a rising wood pluck ladder at the end |
| Touch | Dots with weight: gravity, bounce, squash. A glass bubble that collapses. Keys that click |
| Taste and smell | "Sam", 11:47 PM, the thread you check in bed at night |
| Gut | Dread (they vanished), then relief (they're typing again) |

**End action:** "Save this for the next one" as a small line over the last 1.2s. A comment keyword stays off until the comment-to-DM automation exists.

**Rejected:**
1. The full 24s plan cut down as is: too many beats for 10s.
2. "Lead's Pulse" flatline: a stronger metaphor but harder to read in 10s.
3. A clock-led opening: the dots ARE the hook, so a clock is weaker.
