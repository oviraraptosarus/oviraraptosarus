# Frames for reel ideation (add these to the adhd frame table; pick 5 per run, at least one wild)

| Frame | Vantage prompt |
|---|---|
| **kid with a tablet** | You are a 10 year old who watches 300 videos a day. What makes you stop, laugh or say "wait, what"? You hate explanations and love surprises, sounds and things breaking. |
| **brainrot scroller** | You swipe in 0.7 seconds. Give hooks and visuals that win the first frame with no context. Name the exact thing that stops the thumb. |
| **satisfying loop** | Make the visual itself satisfying: things snapping into place, sorting, filling, clicking, perfect timing, ASMR sound. |
| **character first** | Dot (or a new character) wants something and something blocks it. Show it as a short scene with a face and a reaction, not as a diagram. |
| **before and after** | Two states, one cut. What is the most dramatic, wordless contrast of the same scene? |
| **sound first** | Start from the sound (a buzz, a ding, a silence, a rewind). What picture does that sound demand? |
| **physical metaphor** | Turn the abstract thing into a physical object: a queue of people at a door, a leaking bucket, a phone drowning in notifications, a door that closes. Draw it big. |
| **POV / screen recording** | Show the exact moment on a phone screen a coach lives in: the DM, the typing, the seen mark, the 2am glow. Real UI language, full frame. |
| **tiny stakes, big reaction** | One small moment (a read receipt) blown up with cinematic weight. |
| **format hijack** | Take this month's trending format and bend it to our point. What is the one element of the format we must keep for it to read as the trend? |
| **the villain** | Personify the thing that kills the sale (the Clock, Seen, the Pile, the Empty Chair). Give it a move and a sound. |
| **teach a stranger** | A friend asks "how do I fix this?". What is the one move, shown in 10 seconds, they can copy? |
