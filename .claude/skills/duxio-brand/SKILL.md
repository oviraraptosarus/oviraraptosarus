# DUXIO brand rulebook

**Brand kit:** `brand/duxio/` holds everything this skill points to: logos (SVG, PNG, social avatars, the monogram), fonts, `tokens/tokens.css` and `tokens.json`, SFX, the owner's ElevenLabs voice, the master spec verbatim (`docs/owner-spec-parts-1-to-5.md`), facts, live site copy, the content library and raw research. Read `brand/duxio/README.md` first, and use those files instead of asking the owner again.

DUXIO builds and runs the follow up that moves serious buyers from first interest to a booked sales call, for coaches and consultants selling high ticket offers. Everything below applies to every DUXIO asset. `duxio-website` and `duxio-vsl` add their own rules on top and never relax these.

## 1. Voice
- A calm, grounded infrastructure engineer. A senior partner auditing backend operations. Never a hyped internet marketer or an agency salesman.
- Write like a voice note from a sharp, honest operator. Read every line aloud; if nobody would say it across a coffee table, delete it.
- Short, conversational sentences. Mix three word sentences with natural ones. Paragraphs of one or two lines, three at most.
- Frame: relief and upside, not tech. The client already has good content and an offer. Manual follow up is leaking money while they work.
- If an idea needs more than three sentences, cut it in half.

## 2. Hard copy rules (pre-flight; a violation blocks delivery)
1. **Zero dashes.** No hyphen, en dash or em dash in any customer-facing text: headings, body, bullets, buttons, captions, on-screen video text, VO scripts. Write `follow up`, `check in`, `high ticket`, `24 hours`, `$300 to $800`. Use periods, commas or a new sentence.
2. **No freebies or audit framing.** Never "free audit", "free session", "free consultation", "free review", "free blueprint", cash-back or "we pay you if we waste your time". The meeting is a **Strategy Call** (or Sales Call).
3. **Outcome over activity.** The outcome is paying clients and rescued revenue, not "booked calls" or "calendar clicks".
4. **No public money gate.** Never filter by the coach's income or revenue ("must be making $5,000/mo"). Filter by situation (see §6).
5. **No fake proof.** No client results, testimonials, logos, percentages or screenshots that are not real and supplied by the owner. There are no public client results yet.
6. **No throat-clearing openers:** "In today's fast-paced world", "Imagine a world where", "Picture this", "Look,".

## 3. Banned vocabulary and replacements
| Banned | Use instead |
|---|---|
| GoHighLevel, GHL | our backend system, our private infrastructure (keep the software internal) |
| CRM | one central inbox, one clean dashboard |
| OS, Operating System | backend, system |
| Booking engine | scheduling workflow, calendar routing |
| Pipeline system, coaching and consulting pipeline system | state the plain outcome |
| Blueprint | Book a Strategy Call, Book a Call |
| AI chatbot, automated bot | an instant reply in your voice |
| "Coaches who already get leads" (in a hero) | keep the hero on the upside: "Turn interested leads into paying clients." |

**Plain words over stiff ones (they read as AI):**
- "estimate", not "forecast"
- "use", not "utilize"
- "help", not "facilitate"
- "start", not "commence"
- "about", not "approximately"

When labelling an example number, write "Example. Just an estimate." or "Your numbers will vary."

**Anti-AI blacklist:** delve, tapestry, elevate, streamline, leverage, game-changer, seamless, cutting-edge, revolutionize, robust, paramount, unleash, bespoke. No rhythmic triples ("faster, smarter, better") and no empty filler adjectives. No fake hype, no melodrama.

## 4. The mechanism: always these four steps
1. **Every lead gets an answer.** Every inquiry on Instagram, a form or email gets a reply in under sixty seconds, in the coach's own voice, even while they sleep or coach.
2. **Serious buyers get sorted.** Three short, respectful questions about goals and situation. People who are not ready get pointed to free content. Serious buyers get invited to pick a time.
3. **Follow up for 21 days, plus call reminders.** Short, helpful check ins and stories over three weeks. Reminders 24 hours, 2 hours and 10 minutes before the call so people show up on time.
4. **Quiet leads reopened.** Cold contacts get a short, human check in once a month that restarts conversations with people who were not ready before.

Never call it five stages. Never show more or fewer steps.

## 5. The offer
- **What we sell:** a done for you client acquisition backend that captures, qualifies and follows up with leads until they book a high ticket sales call. We write every message, build the routing, connect the inboxes and run it daily. The client changes nothing about their content or offer.
- **What we don't sell:** websites, software subscriptions, AI chatbots, templates, raw tech setup.
- **Pricing:** $397 one time to build it (live in under 48 hours), then $300 to $800 a month to run it, based on lead volume. Never write "$397/mo"; setup and monthly are separate.
  - Justification frame: we don't charge thousands upfront because we want to earn the monthly partnership. One rescued high ticket client pays for the whole thing.
  - **Website:** may show pricing. **VSL, reels, ads:** never say a price.
- **Strategy Call pitch:** "This is a sales call. We'll pull up your current lead flow, pinpoint where sales are leaking, and show you exactly how we'd run this backend for you. If it makes sense for both of us, we get started. If not, you leave knowing exactly where your leads drop off."

## 6. Who it's for (anti-discrimination qualification)
Filter by situation, never by income. Approved wording:
> "To be clear, this is not for someone who hasn't launched yet and has nobody to talk to. It's for active coaches and consultants who already have people reaching out, commenting or asking about their help, but don't have a dedicated backend to capture and close them."

Form questions (offer price point, monthly inquiries, biggest bottleneck) are operational fit, never a financial status check.
- Fit headings read **"Who this is ideal for"**, never "Who this is for", which sounds like gatekeeping.
- Keep it accessible: offers from about $1,000 and up are welcome. Wherever a demo shows the form, select the "$1,000 to $3,000" option, not a high bracket.

## 7. Claims that may be made (everything else needs the owner's confirmation)
- Reply in under sixty seconds, in the coach's voice; the coach approves the messages before anything goes out.
- Three short questions sort serious buyers; the coach gets a text when someone looks ready.
- Three weeks (21 days) of follow up; reminders 24h, 2h and 10m before the call.
- Quiet leads get a monthly human check in.
- A weekly report.
- Built inside the coach's own accounts; it stays theirs.
- About 30 minutes of the coach's time to set up; live in under 48 hours.
- **Third-party stat (cite on screen):** Harvard Business Review, "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads" (2011): firms that tried to contact leads within an hour were nearly 7 times as likely to qualify them as those that waited longer, and over 60 times as likely as those that waited 24 hours or more.
- **ROI math, only as an illustration:** "If 80 people reach out and this rescues one or two sales at $3,000, that's $3,000 to $6,000 you didn't spend more on ads to get." Label it as an example, never a promise.
- Any example message, name (Maya etc.) or time in a demo is labelled "Example".

## 8. Brand tokens
- Canvas `#FFFFFF`, subtle `#F9FAFB`. Ink `#111113`. Secondary `#4B5563`, muted `#6B7280`.
- Violet `#6F00FF`: primary actions, active states, the one highlight. Never decoration.
- Alert red `#F1283A`: problems, missed revenue, leaks only.
- Obsidian `#0D0E12`: the video frame and focus surfaces.
- Borders `rgba(0,0,0,.08)` to `rgba(0,0,0,.12)`, thin.
- Font: Plus Jakarta Sans 400 to 800 in `brand/duxio/fonts/`; `brand/duxio/tokens/tokens.css` declares them with the colour variables.
- Logos, all in `brand/duxio/logo/`:
  - `svg/duxio-wordmark-{ink,white,violet,violet-gradient}.svg`
  - `svg/duxio-wordmark-parts.svg` (per-letter ids, for animation)
  - `png/*-2400.png`
  - `original/monogram-metal.png` (the hexagon mark)
  - `social/avatar-*.png` (profile pictures)
- Banned look: floating purple-pink gradients, neon glassmorphism blobs, generic SaaS card grids, low-contrast grey text on dark grey cards.

## 9. Pre-flight check (run on every DUXIO deliverable)
1. Search the customer-facing text for `-`, `–`, `—`: zero hits.
2. No banned words (§3), no blacklist words.
3. No "free" offer of any kind; CTA reads "Book a Strategy Call" or "Book a Call".
4. No income gate.
5. Mechanism is exactly the four steps in §4.
6. Every claim is in §7, or the owner confirmed it.
7. Price appears only on the website, written as setup plus monthly.
