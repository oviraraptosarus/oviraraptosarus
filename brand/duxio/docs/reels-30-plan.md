# 30 reels: the plan (weeks 1 to 6)

Made with the `reel-ideation` method (`.claude/skills/reel-ideation/`) on 2026-10-11. This is the second batch, after the 7 launch reels in `reels-launch-7.md`. It replaces the 20 reel slots in `content-calendar-month-1.md` (the Dot series stays as an optional cameo, not the spine).

## How these 30 were chosen

1. **Diverge.** Five isolated parallel branches, each on different angles (kid with a tablet and satisfying loops, brainrot scroller and trend hijack, character and villain, physical metaphor and before and after, POV and sound first). 50 concepts came back (raw file: `source-material/reel-ideas-raw-2026-10-11.json`).
2. **Cut duplicates.** Typing dots (3 versions), hats (3), tools and tabs (5), show rate (4), price (5) and short replies (2) each collapsed to the strongest.
3. **Gates.** Each survivor had to pass: a frame 0 image that works with sound off, a pain first hook, one idea, value without DUXIO, a different visual per beat, a big hero visual (no small cards), and every rule in `duxio-brand`. Concepts that were rule safe but generic were cut.
4. **Balance.** One pain per week. Trend formats up front for reach. Easy builds (difficulty 1) and hard builds (3) spread so no week is all hard. 30 unique keywords, none overlapping the 10 launch keywords.

**Rules that apply to all 30:** faceless, voiceover only, no AI images or video, no zoom filler. Value first, one soft DUXIO mention at most, CTA is 'Comment WORD and I'll DM you', never the word free, no prices, no results, no guarantees, zero dashes, every mock number labelled Example. Voice is an energetic female ElevenLabs voice. Trend formats named here come from search roundups and are unverified: check each in the app the week you post.

- **Day in the Life of Five Minutes** (GAPS): A day timeline is walked hour by hour to find three gaps, and the gaps become reply windows instead of dead time.
- **How It Started, How It's Going: Your Tabs** (LIST): A tab explosion is collapsed into one four column list a coach can build in a spreadsheet tonight.
- **The Dots That Quit** (TYPING): Dot types a real question, the Typing Dots villain steals the letters back one at a time, and Dot only finishes when the coach's reply makes answering easy.
- **Who Is Sam** (NOTE): Dot is a blank contact among thirty identical blank cards until the coach adds one line of context and Dot lights up.

- **The Blind Spot** (RATE): The Blind Spot swallows the dial until the coach counts booked against showed and finds the real show rate.
- **Seven Orbs** (INBOX): The orbs drop one at a time and Dot is hit, until the coach makes one place where every lead lands.
