# DUXIO content strategy: Instagram, Facebook, YouTube (organic, faceless)

Month 1, scripted day by day: `content-calendar-month-1.md`.

Brief (owner answers, 2026-10-11):
- **Starting point:** brand new pages, 0 to 100 followers.
- **Format:** faceless, with a voiceover.
- **Cadence:** 5 reels a week plus 1 YouTube video a week.
- **Paid reach:** no ads. Reach comes from pain point content that coaches and consultants feel, edited so it also hooks the fast scrolling Instagram crowd.

Customer facing lines in this file follow `duxio-brand`: zero dashes, CTA "Book a Strategy Call", no price, no "5 spots", no results, examples labelled "Example".

## 1. Bottom line

1. **Use one funnel.**
   - The reel brings reach.
   - The profile sells the click.
   - A comment or DM triggers our own backend, which answers in under 60 seconds. The page becomes the live demo.
   - The VSL builds trust.
   - Then they book a Strategy Call.
   - We have no client results yet, so **the proof is the experience of being followed up by us.**
2. **Brainrot in the edit, calm in the words.**
   - The visuals are fast and satisfying: slot reel counters, DM notification stacks, money counters, a sound on every action and a visual change every 1.5 to 2.5s.
   - The script stays a calm, sharp operator naming a coach's exact pain.
   - The hook is broad enough for anyone to stop scrolling. The payload is narrow enough that only coaches feel it.
3. **Design every reel for sends.**
   - Instagram has said sends (shares by DM) are one of the strongest signals for reaching people who don't follow you. [verified: Adam Mosseri, 2024 to 2025]
   - Pain that coaches recognise ("this is literally me") gets sent to other coaches.
4. **Content alone rarely books clients from zero followers within 90 days.** [medium]
   - Pair it with 30 to 45 minutes a day of real engagement and warm DMs to coaches who interact.
   - The content makes the profile credible when they check you out.
   - The conversation books the call.

        └─ "Send this to a coach" (reach)         ▼

**Cross-posting**
- Each reel goes to Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts.
- Use the same video with a different caption or title per platform. Shorts get a searchable title, e.g. "How coaches lose leads in DMs".
- Every YouTube video also gives 2 or 3 reels for the next weeks.
- Each reel's strongest frame becomes a carousel or story 3 days later.

**Stories, daily:** one poll or question ("How fast do you reply to DMs?"), plus a reshare of the day's reel. Stories warm up the followers you already have.

**Test hooks with Trial Reels.** Instagram can show a reel to non-followers first. [verified, launched Dec 2024]
- Post 2 hook variants of the same reel.
- Keep the one with the lower skip rate.

## 6. Reel blueprint (the house format)

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0.0s | **Pattern break with sound:** a DM notification drops, a money counter spins, or a "Seen 3h ago" stamp. Text on screen at frame 0 |
| 0 to 2s | **Hook line:** names the coach pain in under 8 words |
| 2 to 20s | **Payload:** one idea, a visual change every 1.5 to 2.5s, a rolling counter or UI demo, captions of 2 to 4 words |
| last 2 to 3s | **One CTA.** Rotate: "Comment REPLY", "Send this to a coach who…", "Book a Strategy Call. Link in bio." The video loops back to frame 0 |

- **Length mix:** 60% at 12 to 25s, 30% at 25 to 40s, 10% at about 60s (the VSL cutdowns).
- **Voice:** energetic female (ElevenLabs, stability 30 to 45, style 25 to 45, speed 1.05 to 1.15).
- **Sound:** SFX 7 to 8 dB under the voice, no chimes.
- **Production:** built with `/make-video` to `editing-standard.md` and `attention-mechanics.md`.
- **Guardrail:** brainrot energy, never cringe. No meme templates that cheapen a premium service, no fake screenshots of real people, no fake wins.

## 10. Phases (90 days)

| Days | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 14 | **Setup and bank** | Profile, bio, highlights. 9 launch posts (3 pinned). Our own keyword backend live. 10 reels banked. YouTube #1 (the VSL) and #2 live |
| 15 to 45 | **Volume and testing** | 5 reels and 1 YouTube a week. 2 hook variants per week via Trial Reels. Weekly review every Sunday |
| 46 to 90 | **Double down** | Turn the top 20% of formats into series. Expand each winning reel into a YouTube deep dive. Recut the VSL into 3 reels. Drop formats that underperform 3 times in a row |
