### Will this sound like a robot talking to my people?

No. We write every message in your own voice and you approve all of it before anything goes out. Your reputation is the whole point.

### Why not just hire an assistant to message people?

An assistant sleeps, forgets and costs more every month. This answers in under a minute, day or night, and never loses a lead.

### How do you stop people from not showing up?

Reminders go out 24 hours, 2 hours and 10 minutes before the call, plus a short note on how you work, so they arrive warm.

### Do I need to learn anything new?

No. We build it, write it and run it. You get a text when someone serious comes in, and a weekly report. Setup takes about 30 minutes of your time.

### Who owns it if we stop working together?

You do. It is built inside your own accounts and your leads stay yours. Nothing is held hostage.
