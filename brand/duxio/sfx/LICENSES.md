# SFX sources
- `*.ogg` (card-*, click*, drop_*, impactGeneric_*, mouseclick1, select_008, switch2): Kenney game audio packs, CC0, bundled with the brag plugin.
- `keypress-*.wav`: brag plugin bundle.
- `hookhit, swell, pulse, tap1-4, confirm2, coin, cash` (.wav): synthesised for the VSL v4 (`edits/duxio-vsl-v4/src/tools/synth.py`), free to use.
- `chime, ctick, haptic, join, ping, rewind, riser, send, snap, sub, tick, whoosh` (.wav): synthesised in this repo (numpy/scipy), free to use.
Music beds are not copied here: brag's "Happy Beats" tracks (`~/.claude/plugins/cache/brag/brag/*/skills/brag/assets/music/`) have an unverified licence. Use them for drafts; use a licensed track or a platform sound for anything published.
