# 1:57 Evergreen Native VSL framework (Charlie Morgan + Hormozi)

Combining Charlie Morgan’s Evergreen Native VSL architecture and Alex Hormozi’s Offer & Value Equation principles, here is the optimal 1-minute 57-second (1:57) VSL Framework designed for high-ticket coaches selling $1,000–$10,000 programs.
The Architecture: Why 1:57 Works

* The 1:58 Hard Ceiling: Keeping the video under 1 minute 58 seconds allows it to be sent natively as an MP4 file inside social messaging apps (Instagram, LinkedIn, Messenger, X) without forcing the prospect to leave the app or click external links.
* Disarming Transparency: Opening by calling out your target avatar and being transparent that it is a sales video instantly drops sales resistance.
* High-Level Positioning: You must never feature-dump technical tools (avoid mentioning "GoHighLevel," "webhooks," or "Twilio"). Instead, sell the destination—stopping lead leakage and doubling booked sales calls.

Phase 1: Disarming Hook + High-Level Offer + Risk Reversal (0:00 – 0:20)

* Objective: Hook the specific avatar, establish immediate transparency, state the core outcome, and introduce a risk-reversing guarantee within the first 20 seconds.
* Psychological Mechanics: Hormozi and Morgan both emphasize that high-ticket buyers hate being tricked. Stating up front that it is a brief sales pitch eliminates skepticism, while the risk reversal lowers the barrier to entry.
* Framework Script:
"If you're watching this, it's because you're a high-ticket coach selling $1,000 to $10,000 programs. Full transparency: this is a quick 1-minute 5-second video explaining how we install an automated client acquisition engine that doubles your booked sales calls—on a complete pay-on-results basis. If we don't increase your booked calls, you don't pay us a single dollar."

Phase 2: Agitate the Core Pain — "The Lead Leak" (0:20 – 0:45)

* Objective: Call out the exact operational nightmare the coach experiences every day.
* Psychological Mechanics: Coaches who already run ads don't need more leads; they are bleeding revenue through manual follow-up delays, slow response times, missed calendar bookings, and messy lead management.
* Framework Script:
"Right now, if you're already generating leads, your biggest bottleneck isn't traffic—it's lead leakage. You are losing thousands in profit every month simply because of delayed follow-ups, no-show appointments, and qualified prospects slipping through the cracks of manual texts or disorganized spreadsheets."

Phase 3: The Unique Mechanism — High Level (0:45 – 1:15)

* Objective: Introduce your proprietary solution as the bridge between their current nightmare and their desired outcome, without revealing low-level software details.
* Psychological Mechanics: Explain what the system accomplishes, not how the code is written. Show how it increases speed to lead, automates reminders, and centralizes their pipeline.
* Framework Script:
"We solve this by installing our plug-and-play Coach Follow-Up Engine. The moment a lead opts in, our system instantly triggers automated SMS and email sequences, captures their info, books them directly onto your calendar 24/7, and sends multi-touch show-up reminders. Your pipeline runs on 100% complete autopilot."

Phase 4: Proof & Value Equation Reinforcement (1:15 – 1:40)

* Objective: Show proof (over Loom screen or slides) and highlight how the offer maximizes likelihood of achievement while minimizing effort/sacrifice.
* Psychological Mechanics: Leverage Hormozi's Value Equation: show that this eliminates the need for manual labor or hiring expensive setters, giving them speed and certainty. Reiterate the guarantee a second time.
* Framework Script:
"Instead of hiring a $50k/year assistant or spending hours manually texting leads, this system captures every opportunity instantly. And again: we legally vow that if we don't fix your lead follow-up and measurably increase your booked calls, you pay nothing."

Phase 5: Low-Friction Call to Action (1:40 – 1:57)

* Objective: Close with a soft, permission-based CTA inviting them to a Free Coach Funnel Audit.
* Psychological Mechanics: Keep the required action as effortless as possible (e.g., "reply with a thumbs up" or "click below").
* Framework Script:
"It's completely low-pressure—keep your credit card in your wallet. If you'd like us to perform a Free Coach Funnel Audit and show you where your pipeline is losing money, drop me a quick thumbs up or click the button below. Send that thumbs up right now, and let's get you booked out!"

Execution Checklist for Maximum Conversions

1. Video Format & Delivery: Record as a horizontal selfie video or a Loom screen recording with your face in the corner. Ensure video and audio quality are crisp.
2. The 20-Second Rule: Confirm that your audited offer and risk-reversing guarantee are clearly stated within the first 20 seconds.
3. No Technical Jargon: Double-check that you haven't mentioned "GoHighLevel," "GHL," "workflows," or "APIs." Keep it focused entirely on business outcomes: recovered revenue and booked sales calls.
4. Physical Thumbs Up: When delivering the CTA at the end of the video, physically raise your thumb up to the camera to reinforce the visual cue.
